Build a ready-to-copy request for an AI assistant to help you study and understand your coursework.

Build your prompt

You choose what you want to make and fill in a few details. The tool writes the prompt. You then paste it into the AI assistant you use, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Claude. Nothing you type here is saved or sent anywhere.

Your prompt

To print from your keyboard, press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac).

What to do next

  1. Open the AI assistant you use.
  2. Paste your prompt into the message box.
  3. Replace the words in [square brackets]. Paste your own class notes where the prompt asks for them, or attach your notes if your assistant lets you.
  4. Send it, then answer the questions without looking at your notes.

Then try asking this next

The best learning often comes from the second question. After the first answer, you can copy one of these.

    Before you send

    Ask yourself: If I use AI for this, am I learning what my instructor wants me to learn? If yes, it is probably fine as a study tool. If no, do not use it.

    • Check the answers against your notes or textbook. AI can make mistakes.
    • For licensing or certification content, check the official, current standards.
    • Check your instructor’s rules about using AI.
    • Do not type your name, student ID, or other personal information.

    Do not use AI:

    • During quizzes or exams.
    • To do graded work that you will turn in as your own.
    • To make citations or sources. AI often invents ones that do not exist.

    If your instructor asks you to say when you used AI

    Some instructors want a short note. Fill in the brackets with your own words.
